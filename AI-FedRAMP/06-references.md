## FedRAMP artifacts and topics to validate against current program materials

When implementing or reviewing an AI feature for a FedRAMP environment, validate the current versions of these materials on the official FedRAMP site or through current agency guidance:

- System Security Plan templates and guidance
- Security Assessment Plan templates and guidance
- Security Assessment Report templates and guidance
- POA&M templates and guidance
- Authorization boundary guidance
- Significant change policies and procedures
- Continuous monitoring guidance and reporting expectations

## Notes on this guide

This documentation is an implementation-oriented guide, not legal advice and not an official FedRAMP interpretation.

FedRAMP guidance, templates, and required processes change over time. Before shipping an AI capability into a federal environment, validate the current requirements with:

- the latest FedRAMP PMO documentation,
- the sponsoring agency,
- your 3PAO if one is involved,
- and your organization's security, privacy, and legal stakeholders.
